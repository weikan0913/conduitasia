We help sport and lifestyle brands succeed in Asia, from the operating side.

Conduit Asia is a brand strategy consultancy focused on sport, footwear, apparel, and lifestyle. Greater China, Japan, Korea, and Southeast Asia each run on distinct channel economics, consumer sentiment, and retail calendars. We work with brand, commercial, and investor teams to shape market entry, brand positioning, category growth, and channel strategy grounded in how each market actually operates.

The Work

We cover the full spectrum of brand and market strategy across sport and consumer lifestyle, helping leadership teams build, reset, and scale their business in Asia or abroad.

Brand and Market Strategy

For brands entering new markets, repositioning, or building out a category. We develop go-to-market plans, brand and assortment architecture, and cross-border expansion strategies for international brands in Asia and Asian brands going global.

Commercial, Channel, and Partnership Strategy

For resolving structural commercial questions and high-stakes moves. This spans direct-to-consumer and wholesale mix, pricing and discounting resets, global-to-local alignment, and structuring commercial partnerships or IP collaborations.

Ongoing Leadership Counsel

For management teams navigating multi-quarter transitions, channel restructuring, or regional expansion. We participate in core commercial reviews on a scheduled basis, bringing current market intelligence to stress-test plans and keep execution on track.

Execution scope. Conduit Asia holds the strategy and commercial narrative. When a direction is set and moves into creative production, media, or local activation, we coordinate alongside your internal teams or regional partners against the same brief.

Research and analysis. Institutional investors and research firms consult us on category structure and the operating reality behind reported numbers, from channel inventory health to discounting pressure and brand momentum.

FAQ

What does Conduit Asia do?

Conduit Asia is a brand strategy consultancy for sport, footwear, apparel, and lifestyle. We work with leadership teams on market entry, brand positioning, category growth, channel resets, and ongoing commercial counsel, alongside research consultations for institutional investors.

How do engagements start?

Send us a short email outlining the business decision on the table and the context around it. We will read it and schedule an initial call to discuss the options. Write to wei.kan@conduitasia.com.

Do you take on execution work?

We hold the strategy and commercial judgment. When a direction is set and requires execution, we can coordinate with your internal teams or regional execution partners against the same brief.
