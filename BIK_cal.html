China BIK Housing Allowance Tax Calculator for Expats 外籍员工中国 BIK 房租免税试算器

For expats working in China, housing benefits (BIK) can be exempt from individual income tax up to a limit. Enter salary and rent to see how annual after-tax net cash changes with rent—find the tax-free cap, then choose how much rent suits your lifestyle.

Who this applies to (confirm you fit)

  • Region: Mainland China. These rules are under China individual income tax (IIT). BIK regimes elsewhere differ entirely; this tool does not apply.
  • Who: Foreign (including HK/Macao/Taiwan) employees working in China without a domicile there, and who are tax residents (183 days or more in China in a tax year). Non-residents use different monthly rates; this calculator does not apply.
  • How the exemption works: Housing rent and similar allowances received in kind or on a reimbursement basis can be tax-free up to a “reasonable” amount (invoices or payment vouchers required). In practice, Chinese tax authorities often treat “not exceeding 30%–35% of monthly salary” as reasonable, so this tool offers 30% and 1/3 as common caps. This preferential policy is currently extended through end of 2027. Tax residents must choose either BIK exemption or special additional deductions—not both.
  • Not limited to one employer: The logic was modeled on a large multinational’s BIK practice but applies equally to other employers offering BIK housing benefits.
  • This is a free, public conceptual calculator, not formal tax or legal advice. Final figures depend on company policy, local tax bureau interpretation, and professional advisors.
