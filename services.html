Services

Conduit Asia advises sport and lifestyle brands from the operating side. Asia is not one market. Mainland China, Japan and Korea, and Southeast Asia each have their own channel structure, consumer mood, and retail pace.

Work with brand and commercial teams takes three forms. You can start with any of them. Commercial narrative sits beside that work. Conversations with investors and research firms are separate.

With brand teams

Three ways to work together.

At a decision point, another report is rarely the missing piece. What helps is someone who understands the constraints on resources, and who will offer an independent, practical view from having operated in the market. Where a regional strategy team is not yet in place, we can sit with senior leaders as that outside view. The view stays independent of any conclusion the team has already chosen.

Decision

Key decision support

When a decision is already on the table.

This is for a question that needs a decision soon. A global category direction may need to be read against the local market. A leadership team may be weighing the mix of direct retail and wholesale.

We read the business context you share, set it against competitive benchmarking and the channel and consumer evidence that can be checked, and write an independent view. The writing is there to clarify the decision with your team.

Suited to leaders who want that view before they commit to a larger effort.

Market

New-market assessment

Before a larger commitment.

This is for a direction that needs a structured look, and not yet a large spend. An Asian brand weighing markets outside the region, including how the story translates and which channels fit. Or a collaboration whose commercial value has to be judged over more than one season.

We agree the question and the research boundary first. The strategy briefing covers market evidence that can be checked, channel logic, and how price is behaving in market. We then sort the next actions with you by priority.

Suited to teams that want the priorities clear before they scale the effort.

Advisory

Ongoing advisory

Through a longer stretch of change.

This is for a brand in a period of channel restructuring, category shift, or coordination across markets. What helps is not a one-time answer. It is an outside view that stays in the conversation.

At an agreed rhythm, we join core meetings of the brand or commercial team, bring current observations on Asian markets, and review merchandise and channel plans with the team, so the strategy stays workable as the business moves.

Suited to leadership teams that want that view present while the plan is being carried out.

Story

Narrative and content

Make the commercial logic travel.

We help a brand explain the logic of a move in Asia, such as a channel innovation or a move into a new category, to people inside the company and to industry readers, investors, and consumers who follow the business. The aim is a narrative that holds, rather than a standard product press release.

From the Inside can carry a sponsored essay or a commissioned deep read. Sponsored and brand-collaboration pieces are labeled, and they are kept separate from independent editorial. Favorable coverage is not for sale. Published analysis is not a client project. Chinese-language public writing is on Zhihu and Xiaohongshu.

Suited to brands speaking with industry decision-makers, investors, and readers who follow the category.

When the work turns to execution. Conduit Asia concentrates on the commercial judgment and the strategic direction. Once that direction is set, and the work moves into day-to-day operations or creative communications, we work from the same brief with your existing team and with regional partners. Strategy stays with us. Execution sits with the people best placed to carry it out. How far we stay alongside that work is agreed for the engagement. Engagements are paid, and the scope is agreed in writing before work begins.

Investors and research

An operator read, from indicators that are available.

Financial statements in this category often lag what is happening at retail. We speak with investment and research teams from the operating side, using indicators that are actually available. That includes public disclosures, discount pressure that can be observed, the product cycle, and whether attention is turning into demand. We do not imply access to private inventory, and we do not suggest an investment outcome can be known in advance.

The usual forms are a scheduled conversation around a reporting window, or commissioned research on a brand, a category, or a market. Conduit Asia does not provide investment advice, does not rate securities, and does not broker transactions.
