Publication

From the Inside

Our public research, market intelligence, and cultural analysis live on Substack under From the Inside.

The desk covers Asia from the operating side. We bring together China macroeconomic context, rigorous commercial analysis, and the evolving social and athletic culture across Greater China, Northeast Asia, and Southeast Asia.

Readers receive at least two weekday digests on most weeks, alongside a longer weekly analysis timed to earnings releases, policy announcements, or major retail movements. The desk also shares adapted, real-world analytical frameworks derived from our advisory mandates.

Editorial coverage is coordinated with our Chinese-language imprint, 场内, through a syndicated model that shares core strategic frameworks while tailoring specific coverage to respective English and Chinese audiences.
